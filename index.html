The Free 14-0 IPL Team Generator & Roster Builder

Can You Go 14-0? is a free browser game built around a simple dare: use the draft wheel — a random IPL team generator that deals you a franchise and an era on every spin — to build an all-time XI of legends, then simulate a full 14-match league stage. Win them all and you've done what almost no real IPL team ever has: a perfect 14-0.

How to play

  1. Spin the wheel. The generator lands on a franchise + era combo (say, 2016-19 RCB or 2020-22 Mumbai Indians) and shows you that squad's players.
  2. Draft one player from the board and place them in your lineup. Skips are limited, so spend them wisely.
  3. Fill all five slots — Opener, Middle-order, Wicketkeeper, Pace, Spin — chasing the highest Team Rating you can build.
  4. Pick your captain. Each captain boosts a different style of XI.
  5. Simulate the 14-match season and see how far your record carries — then take an unbeaten team into the playoffs.

Frequently asked questions

What is Can You Go 14-0?

It's a free IPL roster builder and season simulator. You draft an all-time XI of legends from every era using a randomized draft wheel, then simulate a 14-match season. The goal — and the name — is finishing a perfect 14-0.

How does the 14-0 IPL team generator work?

Every spin randomly generates a franchise and an era (like 2012-15 Chennai Super Kings or 2023-25 Gujarat Titans), and you draft one player from that combination. Repeat until all five starting spots are filled. No two runs deal the same boards, so every roster is unique.

What's the best XI to go 14-0?

Team Rating is the biggest lever — draft the highest-overall players you can. But raw rating isn't everything: the simulator also rewards balance across all five stat categories (runs, strike rate, wickets, economy, fielding) and a captain whose system matches your XI. A five-superstar lineup that's thin in one category will still drop matches a balanced team wouldn't.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Once a day, every player in the world gets the same draft board and the same special rule — era locks, banned franchises, or building around a locked legend. You get one attempt per day, streaks for consecutive clears, and a global daily leaderboard. See the Daily Challenge archive for all the rules.

Is Can You Go 14-0 free?

Yes — completely free, no download, no sign-up. It runs in your browser on any device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game is fully responsive and plays the same on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
